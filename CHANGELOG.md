# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Fixed most saloon doors staying. 1.0.0 only caught the ones spawned at the Food Court's doorways. The rest, in the shacks and at the start room's entrance, are built into their rooms, and are now taken out once the level has generated. Still host-only: the host removes them through the game's own door-breaking, so they're gone for everyone.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- The saloon doors in Sunstone Valley Resort are gone, so those doorways are always open. Its other doors stay.
- Only the host needs the mod. The doors are never spawned, so they're gone for everyone in the lobby, with or without the mod.
- `Enabled` switches it off, and `PrefabNames` sets which doorway props to leave out.
